# Getting help with GitTree

| You need | Go to |
|---|---|
| How to do something | The [documentation](https://gittree.app/en/docs), then [Discussions › Q&A](https://github.com/git-tree-app/gittree/discussions/categories/q-a) |
| Something is broken | The [bug report form](https://github.com/git-tree-app/gittree/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml), or **Report a Bug** in the app (it fills in your version and OS) |
| GitTree crashed | **Crash Reports…** in the command palette, then the [crash report form](https://github.com/git-tree-app/gittree/issues/new?template=crash_report.yml) |
| Help with your account, organizations or invitations | The [help form](https://github.com/git-tree-app/gittree/issues/new?template=support_request.yml), or **Contact Support** in the app |
| A security problem | Privately, as [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) describes |

Issues and discussions are public: never post tokens, passwords, one-time codes or private repository details. Something you can't describe without them: e-mail [info@gittree.app](mailto:info@gittree.app).
